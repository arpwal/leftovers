# What's new in Leftovers

Written by Claude from each release's changes, in plain words.

## 1.3.0 · September 26, 2026

- New Scheduled section lists all your background jobs in one place.
- See when each job runs next and if it's working.
- Run, pause, resume, or remove a job with one click.
- Open a job's log or find its file quickly.
- Jobs made by coding agents are clearly labelled.

## 1.2.0 · September 26, 2026

- Leftovers now shows in the Dock, and you can hide it.
- Click any row to select it, in every list.
- Double-click a row to open its folder or app.
- Numbers show up piece by piece instead of all at once.
- See how fast the last check ran in the sidebar.

## 1.1.4 · September 26, 2026

- The download window now looks nicer to install from.
- Drag the app onto Applications with a clear arrow.

## 1.1.3 · September 26, 2026

- Small fixes and polish.

## 1.1.2 · September 26, 2026

- Logging out, restarting, or installing updates no longer gets blocked by Leftovers.

## 1.1.1 · September 26, 2026

- Small fixes and polish.

## 1.1.0 · September 26, 2026

- Leftovers now checks for new versions and updates itself.
- Choose "Check for Updates" from the menu any time.
- Turn automatic update checks on or off in Settings.
- Only updates signed by us can be installed.

## 1.0.1 · September 26, 2026

- Small fixes and polish.

## 1.0.0 · September 26, 2026

- Finds memory your apps and coding agents left running.
- Cleans it up with one click, never touching macOS itself.
- Quit apps the normal way, right from the list.
- Quit a coding agent session and everything it started.
- See your memory at a glance from the menu bar.
